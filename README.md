## Hi, I'm Christopher.

I'm a developer from Scotland. I run [kirigoe](https://kirigoe.uk/), a one-person studio making small, focused apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad in SwiftUI. Pay once and keep it: no subscriptions, ads, accounts or tracking.

You can find me elsewhere on the web [at my website](https://kirigoe.uk/), where I also write [a journal](https://kirigoe.uk/blog/).

### Projects

Here's what I'm working on:

#### Apps

* [CueID](https://kirigoe.uk/cueid/) &mdash; turn DJ mixes, radio shows and podcasts into timestamped tracklists. Mac, iPhone and iPad, [on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/cueid-tracklists-for-dj-mixes/id6762413993).
* [Kanji Detective](https://kirigoe.uk/kanji-detective/) &mdash; solve a daily kanji mystery, where every wrong guess is a clue. iPhone and iPad, [on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/kanji-detective-japanese-jlpt/id6816779165).
* [Remux Doctor](https://kirigoe.uk/remux-doctor/) &mdash; know what's inside every 4K remux, check it against your setup and fix it losslessly. Coming soon to the Mac App Store.
